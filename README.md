# Stock Market Simulator

A minimalist, high-stakes financial simulation built with a focus on elegant design and fluid interactions. Experience the volatility of the market through a refined, "Racing Green" and "Gold" aesthetic[cite: 1].

---

## The Aesthetic
The interface is designed with a "Luxe Serif" style, utilizing **Didot** and **Playfair Display** typography to evoke the feeling of a premium trading floor[cite: 1].

*   **Racing Green & Gold:** A sophisticated palette inspired by heritage luxury[cite: 1].
*   **Dynamic Visualization:** A sleek, vertical bar chart that tracks market fluctuations in real-time[cite: 1].
*   **Minimalist Feedback:** Subtle, fading status messages and smooth CSS transitions[cite: 1].

---

## 🕹️ How to start
The simulator starts you with a **$100 bankroll**[cite: 1]. The goal is simple: buy low, sell high.

1.  **Buy ($10):** Acquire a stock for a flat fee. This reduces your bank balance and increases your portfolio[cite: 1].
2.  **Monitor:** Watch the vertical graph. The height represents the current market value (0% to 100%)[cite: 1].
3.  **Sell:** Liquidate your holdings.
    *   **Profit:** Selling when the bar is above the **50% breakeven point**[cite: 1].
    *   **Loss:** Selling when the bar is below 50%[cite: 1].

---

## 🛠️ Technical Highlights
*   **Volatility Engine:** Uses a random percentage-change algorithm to simulate market unpredictability[cite: 1].
*   **Cubic-Bezier Animations:** The graph utilizes custom easing (`0.175, 0.885, 0.32, 1.275`) for organic, lifelike movement[cite: 1].
*   **Responsive Parallax:** Features a subtle mouse-tracking effect for added depth[cite: 1].
*   **Pure Stack:** Built entirely with vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript—no external dependencies required[cite: 1].
